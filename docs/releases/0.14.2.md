# v0.14.2 source release record — 2026-11-13

[← Documentation](../README.md) · [Current installation](../getting-started.md) · [Maintained memory](../agent-memory.md)

This patch improves managed-memory failure isolation and extends the existing
explicit offline archive transaction for retained committed publication owners.
It preserves useful proposals separately from operational blockers. It does not
reset memory, enable reflection, grant permissions or automatically repair a
profile when loaded or installed.

## Avoid nested preparation blockers

Preparation checks canonical lock presence before namespace reclamation and again
before creating a durable plan. An observed lock refuses preparation while leaving
the ready proposal intact, rather than creating another blocking journal.
Reclamation preserves journals and proposals while a canonical lock is observed,
with repeated absence checks before selected removals.

These authority-checked checks are advisory, not atomic exclusion or publication
authority. Native exclusive acquisition and existing sealed contention settlement
remain responsible for races. Draft reads and edits retain their ordinary authority
and revision fences.

A genuinely controller-local preparation that drained without ever admitting
publication may settle only its own ownerless record even under another operation's
retained lock. Current authority and unchanged persisted evidence remain mandatory.
This path never releases the other lock, reconstructs a lost executor reference,
activates a proposal or turns a generic drain into publication proof.

## Recover retained committed owners without inventing uncertainty

The explicit offline operator additionally accepts:

- A standalone retained committed lock owner, verified against actual committed
  state, while preserving unrelated terminal history and its proposal bytes/state.
- That verified owner plus exactly one independently bound ownerless prepared
  operation at actual HEAD: sequence zero, exact prepared receipt, unchanged
  configuration/ledger and an intact ready proposal. The prepared operation stays
  truthfully classified as prepared, not relabelled unknown.

Both additions require exact current root/lock identities; they do not inherit the
older unknown composite's narrow historical device-only exception. Additional
unresolved operations and unrelated CAS refuse. In the committed-plus-prepared
composite, prepared-target staging refuses; only the committed owner's exact typed
empty staging directory may pass through the archive and quarantine gates, while
nonempty or nested owner staging refuses. Standalone committed-owner recovery
retains its bound staging-directory archive/quarantine handling. Unrelated staging
retains the existing strict applicability rules.

The complete independently verified archive, exact inspection fingerprint,
cooperative offline assurance, holder checks and reviewed crash-resume protocol
remain mandatory. Only selected bound blockers move. The prepared proposal is
archived before becoming a conflict in the active namespace; the committed owner's
proposal and unrelated terminal journals remain unchanged. Completion preserves
HEAD, configuration, raw index and committed knowledge. A quiet process or clean
tree alone is not executor-drain or offline-assurance proof.

## Closed recovery diagnostics and verification boundaries

The native operator reports identity-branded `recovery-layout`, `recovery-journal`,
`recovery-lock` and `recovery-artifacts` phases alongside existing verification
categories. Only source-owned identities and closed categories cross the boundary;
exception text, arbitrary error properties and corpus bytes do not become diagnostics.

Disposable regressions cover refusal without a new journal, ready-proposal and
retained-owner evidence preservation, real local retirement followed by a fresh
successful publication, foreign/revoked/missing proof, standalone/composite
archive and resume, and exact identity/config/proposal/staging refusal controls.
The combined release gate includes source and public-SDK tiers; these synthetic
checks do not establish every OS/interleaving, semantic learning or human acceptance.

This is a GitHub source release with an inspected tagged archive and SHA-256
manifest, not npm publication or a license grant. The package remains
`private: true` / `UNLICENSED`. Profiles, private knowledge, transcripts, runtime
evidence and retrospectives stay outside the release payload. Publishing does not
install, reload sessions, apply recovery, change models/policy or close human-owned
product tasks.
